# Utility examples

Reviewed on October 9, 2026. All conversations are fictional New Jersey examples, not live eligibility determinations. MoreTime first asks for details, then the sample user supplies their location and circumstances before the assistant explains a local protection. The jurisdiction is also stated beside the source link. Rules need rechecking before any live use.

| Scenario | Official source | Basis and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shutoff warning | [NJBPU assistance programs](https://www.nj.gov/bpu/assistance/programs/) | Winter Termination Program: November 15–March 15; qualifying hardship can include unemployment. Customers must contact their utility to request protection. Provider coverage and eligibility need confirmation. |
| Payment demanded in full | [NJBPU Utility Customer Bill of Rights, item 4](https://www.nj.gov/bpu/assistance/rights/) | At least one deferred payment plan per year, taking finances into account, while new charges stay current. A particular payment amount is proposed, not guaranteed. |
| Disputed charge | [NJBPU Utility Customer Bill of Rights, item 5](https://www.nj.gov/bpu/assistance/rights/) | Dispute-related shutoff protection during the utility investigation depends on paying undisputed charges. The page also gives the regulator contact route for unresolved issues. |

The [NJBPU Winter Termination Program announcement](https://www.nj.gov/bpu/newsroom/2025/approved/20251114.html) also explains that protection does not cancel accumulated utility bills. Its specific 2025–2026 season is historical; the demonstration uses the annually recurring dates from the current assistance-program page.

Draft requests and follow-up wording are original illustrative text. This source set demonstrates the intended workflow and does not establish nationwide coverage or a production retrieval service.

The dispute example attaches `example-utility-bill.pdf`, generated with its SVG thumbnail from `sample-bill.json`. The provider, account, and charges are fictional. Both files are marked as samples, and no real uploads are collected. The conversation confirms $286 due October 20, 2026, and an estimated meter reading before drafting a review request; an estimate is not treated as proof of a billing error.
